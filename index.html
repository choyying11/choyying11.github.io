About Me

Business Intelligence Specialist

I enjoy understanding the business process behind the data, solving data-quality issues, and building solutions that help teams work with reliable information.

Analytical thinkingProblem solving Data qualityWorkflow automation

My journey in data began at Eco-Shop Marketing, where I worked as a Data Analyst. Working with operational data helped me build a strong foundation in data preparation, analysis, and business problem-solving. I gained experience automating data-processing workflows and developing dashboards to support inventory management and business decisions. This showed me how reliable data and effective analysis can improve business operations.

I later joined Carro Technology as a Business Intelligence Specialist, where my work expanded beyond traditional data analysis into business intelligence and data workflows. Working with SQL, Google BigQuery, Tableau, Python, and Apache Airflow gave me hands-on experience in data transformation, workflow automation, and pipeline development. I also worked on data migration and troubleshooting data discrepancies, which strengthened my understanding of data quality and reliability throughout the data workflow.

Over time, I became increasingly interested in what happens behind the dashboards: how data is extracted from source systems, transformed into reliable datasets, validated, and delivered for downstream analytics. Troubleshooting issues across reporting and pipeline layers further strengthened my interest in data engineering.

Today, I am focused on growing towards Analytics Engineering, combining my experience in business intelligence and data analytics with my interest in building reliable, maintainable data workflows. I aim to develop data solutions that make information more accessible, improve reporting efficiency, and help business teams make better-informed decisions.
